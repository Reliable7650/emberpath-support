隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日

你的记录留在设备上

戒断之路无需注册。我们不通过本应用收集、上传、出售或共享你的个人数据。本应用不接入广告、追踪、使用分析或第三方数据收集服务。

保存什么，为什么保存

习惯名称、旅程及中断记录、个人纪录、余烬、心愿、坚持理由、应对方法、纪念与语言偏好，都保存在本机的应用数据中，用于显示进度、保存历史与纪念，以及安排本地提醒。我们无法读取或代你恢复这些记录。

设备备份

本应用不提供账户或独立云端同步服务。如果你启用了 Apple 的设备备份，应用数据可能包含在系统备份中，由你的 Apple 账户和备份设置管理。

通知

允许通知后，iOS 会在本机安排旅程、纪录和心愿提醒。心愿名称只有在你开启相应的私密文字选项后才会显示在提醒中。你可以随时在 iPhone 系统设置中关闭通知，不影响使用记录功能。

数据保留与删除

记录会保留在设备上，直到你在应用中删除可删除的内容，或在 iPhone 设置中删除本应用及其数据。删除应用会失去本机记录。已有系统备份需在 Apple 的备份设置中单独管理。由于我们不持有这些记录，无法从服务器访问、导出或删除它们。

支持沟通与本网站

如果你主动联系支持人员，你发送的信息将用于处理该次咨询。请不要发送不必要的习惯名称、心愿或私密笔记。本静态网站不设置广告、分析脚本或追踪 Cookie；网站托管服务可能为交付页面与维护安全处理 IP 地址等常规访问记录。

联系开发者

开发者：Xi'an Heikuan Technology Co., Ltd.
隐私咨询：privacy@zhuazhuashu.com。如果你主动发送个人信息，可通过同一邮箱咨询更正或删除相关沟通记录。

政策变更

如果数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策及其日期。使用问题可参阅使用帮助。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Your records stay on your device

EmberPath requires no account. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal data through the app. The app includes no advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party data collection services.

What is stored and why

Habit names, journeys, interruptions, personal records, Embers, Wishes, reasons, coping notes, keepsakes, and language preferences are stored in the app's data on your device. They support progress displays, history, keepsakes, and local reminders. We cannot read or restore these records for you.

Device backups

The app provides no account or separate cloud sync service. If you enable Apple device backups, app data may be included according to your Apple account and backup settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, iOS schedules journey, record, and Wish reminders locally. Wish names appear in reminders only when you enable the corresponding private-text option. You can turn notifications off in iPhone Settings at any time without losing access to the app's tracking features.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete available items in the app or delete the app and its data in iPhone Settings. Deleting the app removes its local records. Existing device backups must be managed separately in Apple's backup settings. Because we do not hold your records, we cannot access, export, or delete them from a server.

Support correspondence and this website

If you contact support, the information you send is used to handle your inquiry. Please avoid sending unnecessary habit names, Wishes, or private notes. This static website sets no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. Its hosting provider may process routine request records, including IP addresses, to deliver pages and maintain security.

Contact the developer

Developer: Xi'an Heikuan Technology Co., Ltd.
Privacy inquiries: privacy@zhuazhuashu.com. If you voluntarily send personal information, use the same address to ask about correcting or deleting the related correspondence.

Changes to this policy

If data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. For help using the app, see Support.
