常见问题

旅程如何前进？

开始记录后，坚持时间会持续累计，无需每天打开应用。旅人和旅程卡牌按坚持时长前进与解锁。中断后可重新开始，过去的旅程记录会保留。

余烬、提灯与心愿有什么关系？

每次抵挡住冲动，点按“我挺过来了”记录一枚余烬。累计余烬可解锁提灯。心愿支持连续天数或累计次数目标，并可写下坚持理由和应对方法。

为什么没有收到通知？

请在 iPhone 系统设置中检查本应用的通知权限，以及专注模式和通知摘要设置。通知是可选的；关闭通知不会影响旅程记录。

记录会同步到其他设备吗？

本应用没有账户或独立云端同步服务。记录保存在本机；系统设备备份受你的 Apple 账户和备份设置管理。删除应用会失去本机记录。

无法读取记录时怎么办？

请保留本应用及其数据，不要卸载，以免失去本机原始记录。反馈时提供应用版本、iOS 版本和出现问题的步骤即可，无需发送习惯名称、心愿或私密笔记。

如何切换语言？

打开应用右上角的设置，可跟随系统，或选择简体中文和英文。切换语言不会改写你输入的内容。

它可以治疗成瘾吗？

戒断之路是自我记录与习惯管理工具，不提供成瘾诊断或治疗，不能替代专业医疗或心理健康服务。需要健康建议时请咨询合格的专业人员；紧急危险请联系当地急救服务。

有关本机记录、通知和删除方式，请阅读隐私政策。

How does my journey progress?

Once you start, your streak keeps counting without opening the app daily. Your traveler and journey cards progress with time. You can restart after an interruption while keeping past journeys.

How do Embers, lanterns, and Wishes work?

Tap I Made It Through after resisting an urge to record an Ember. Collecting Embers unlocks lanterns. Wishes can use consecutive-day or total-count goals, with reasons and coping plans you choose.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Check this app's notification permission, Focus modes, and Scheduled Summary in iPhone Settings. Notifications are optional; turning them off does not affect journey records.

Do records sync across devices?

The app has no account or separate cloud sync service. Records are stored locally. Apple device backups follow your Apple account and backup settings. Deleting the app removes local records.

What if my records cannot be read?

Keep the app and its data installed to preserve the original local records. When reporting an issue, include the app version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce it. You do not need to share habit names, Wishes, or private notes.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings at the top right of the app. Choose Follow System, Simplified Chinese, or English. Changing the display language keeps your entered text unchanged.

Can the app treat addiction?

EmberPath supports self-tracking and habit management. It does not diagnose or treat addiction and does not replace professional medical or mental health care. Consult a qualified professional for health advice. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
